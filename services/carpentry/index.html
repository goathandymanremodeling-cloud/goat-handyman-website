Professional Carpentry Services on Long Island

GOAT installs finish carpentry throughout individual rooms, hallways, and entire floors. If you're replacing flooring or interior doors, we can fit the baseboards, crown molding, and casing together so the profiles carry from one room into the next. We also build shelving for alcoves and storage areas, repair interior stairs, and install railings. When a home has original woodwork you want to keep, we use the existing trim as the reference for the new pieces.

What's Included

  • Whole-room and multi-room baseboard installation
  • Door and window casing replacement
  • Built-in shelving and storage carpentry
  • Interior stair and railing repair or replacement
  • Damaged and rotted wood trim replacement

Frequently Asked Questions

Small dents, nail holes, and localized damage can often be filled, sanded, and refinished when the surrounding material is sound. Replacement is usually more appropriate for extensively deteriorated, swollen, or badly split trim. The decision also depends on whether the existing profile can be matched and whether the finish will be painted or stained.

PVC trim can be a practical option for nonstructural exterior trim because it resists moisture absorption and rot. It is commonly used around windows, doors, and other exposed areas. However, replacing wood with PVC does not fix leaks behind the trim. Flashing, drainage, fastening, and allowance for material movement still need proper attention.
