Professional Deck Repair & Installation on Long Island

Worn deck boards can hide a larger issue, while a deck with sound framing may only need a new surface, stairs, and railings. GOAT checks the framing beneath the walking surface before quoting the project. We can resurface an existing deck, rebuild deteriorated sections, or replace the deck when the underlying structure is unsuitable for reuse. For new decks, we plan the walking area, stair access, and railing layout together.

The condition of the supporting structure matters more than the appearance of the deck boards. Localized surface damage may be repairable, while deterioration in posts, beams, joists, connections, or footings can require more extensive work. An inspection should assess the entire deck, including railings and stairs, before deciding whether repair, resurfacing, or replacement is appropriate.
